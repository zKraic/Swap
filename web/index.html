0.6.0 release notes and checksum ↗
What does it install?

Applicable Display drivers from your configured Windows Update service. It may offer an older driver than the manufacturer. Full vendor suites, PhysX and optional applications are not installed automatically.

Do I need to reboot?

Not to open Swap or run diagnostics. Windows may require a reboot after changing drivers. Swap saves a continuation session and preserves pending packages. Remote sessions and pending servicing restarts block driver changes.

Which PCs are supported?

The preview targets Windows 10/11 x64, PowerShell 5.1, Windows Update and at least 4 GB free plus driver space. Internet is needed for automatic downloads. Physical hardware compatibility still needs testing.

Can I improve or redistribute it?

Yes, under GPL-3.0-or-later, including commercial reuse. Distributed modifications must retain required notices and corresponding source. Forks must not impersonate KraicDev or claim to be official releases.

Does it replace Display Driver Uninstaller?

For a brand change, yes, without Safe Mode: Swap runs after the old card is removed, decides with hardware evidence, keeps chipset, network and PhysX, backs everything up and can undo. On identical copies with the official AMD driver it left less behind than DDU 18.1.5.3 (comparison). DDU is an independent project, not included.
